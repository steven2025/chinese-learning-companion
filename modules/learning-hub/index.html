01

学生使用指南 Student Guide

  1. 使用邀请码登录。Sign in with an invitation code.
  2. 选择教材、课程和辅助语言。Select a textbook, lesson, and support language.
  3. 进入学习并查看个人记录。Start learning and review your progress.
02

教师使用指南 Teacher Guide

  1. 进入教学工作台。Open the Teaching Workspace.
  2. 建立课程并管理学生。Create courses and manage students.
  3. 查看写作与班级学情。Review writing and class insights.
03

课程学习 Course Learning

  1. 按课程结构切换学习部分。Move through each lesson section.
  2. 使用播放、跟读和练习功能。Use playback, repetition, and exercises.
  3. 需要时开启母语辅助。Enable native-language aid when needed.
04

AI伴学功能 AI Learning Assistance

  1. 在当前学习内容中打开AI辅助。Open AI support in the current activity.
  2. 提交语音、书写或练习答案。Submit speech, writing, or exercise answers.
  3. 根据反馈再次练习。Practice again using the feedback.
